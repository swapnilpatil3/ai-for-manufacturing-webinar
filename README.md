# AI for Manufacturing Landing Page
**Manvian Practical Candidate Assessment — Task 1**

## 📌 Executive Overview
This is a modern, high-converting B2B landing page engineered to sell and drive registrations for an executive masterclass titled:
**"From Factory Floor to Autonomous Plants: Deploying AI That Actually Drives ROI"** presented by **Manvian**.

Designed with industrial aesthetic precision (dark cyber-industrial theme with emerald `#10B981` and cyan `#06B6D4` accents, crisp typography, and glassmorphism cards), it addresses the primary pain points of modern plant managers and operations executives: **unplanned downtime, defect escape rates, and brownfield integration complexity**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Interactive Capabilities

1. **Live Urgency Countdown Timer**:
   - Dynamic JavaScript countdown targeting Thursday, December 10, 2026 at 2:00 PM EST.
   - Computes days, hours, minutes, and seconds in real time.

2. **Interactive Industrial AI ROI & Downtime Savings Calculator**:
   - Users can drag sliders for **Number of Production Lines** (1–40), **Downtime Cost ($/hour)** ($2k–$60k), and **Historical Downtime Hours** (4–40 hrs/month).
   - Dynamically calculates the estimated net dollar recovery based on a 38% predictive anomaly reduction model.

3. **High-Converting Hero Registration Form**:
   - Collects Name, Work Email, Company/Plant Name, and Job Title.
   - Dual-tier selection: **Standard Free Pass** vs. **VIP Executive Pass (with 1-on-1 Plant AI Readiness Audit)**.
   - Client-side validation with real-time feedback.

4. **Digital VIP Boarding Pass & Calendar Sync Modal**:
   - On form submission, generates a custom animated digital badge/ticket with attendee name, unique ticket ID (`MANV-xxxx`), and event details.
   - Provides **"Add to Google Calendar"** with pre-populated event details.
   - Generates and downloads a valid `.ics` calendar invitation file for Apple Calendar, Outlook, and Microsoft Teams directly from browser memory.

5. **Technical Curriculum & Agenda Breakdown**:
   - 5 structured tracks covering Edge AI topologies, Vibration & Acoustic anomaly detection, High-speed Neural Computer Vision QA, GenAI SOP Copilots, and Live Telemetry Demystification.

6. **World-Class Faculty & Industry Credibility**:
   - 4 speaker profiles with industrial backgrounds (Ex-Tesla, Siemens, MIT, Tier-1 automotive).
   - Social proof metrics, attendee brand logos (Siemens, Bosch, Honeywell, Caterpillar, Boeing), and verified customer testimonials.

7. **Interactive FAQ Accordion**:
   - Smooth animated toggles answering executive questions regarding technical depth, recordings, team registrations, and prerequisites.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **HTML5 & Semantic Markup**
- **Tailwind CSS (via CDN)**: Modern styling, responsive utility classes, custom color palette.
- **Lucide Icons (via CDN)**: Crisp SVG icons for industrial tech aesthetics.
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Zero build steps or heavy dependencies required. Runs directly out of the box in any browser.
- **Fonts**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* for clean B2B legibility and *Space Grotesk* for technical headings.
